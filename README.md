# Customer Churn Analysis for a Telecommunications Company

## Overview
This project analyzes customer churn for a telecommunications company named SpeedyCall. The aim was to identify customer groups with higher churn risk and provide business recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Dataset
The analysis was based on customer churn data containing 7,043 customers.

## Objectives
- Analyze customer churn patterns by contract type and payment method.
- Identify high-risk customer groups.
- Test whether contract type and payment method are statistically associated with churn.
- Provide business recommendations to reduce churn.

## Methods Used
- Descriptive analysis
- Bar charts
- Percentage component bar charts
- Chi-square tests of independence
- Categorical data analysis

## Key Findings
- 1,869 customers churned, while 5,174 customers stayed with the company.
- Month-to-month customers had the highest churn rate at 42.7%.
- Electronic check users had the highest churn rate by payment method at 45.3%.
- Chi-square tests showed that both contract type and payment method were significantly associated with customer churn.

## Business Recommendations
- Encourage customers to move from month-to-month contracts to longer-term contracts.
- Promote automatic payment methods such as bank transfer or credit card.
- Offer loyalty benefits or upgrade options for high-risk customer groups.
